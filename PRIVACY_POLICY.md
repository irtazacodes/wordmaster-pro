# Privacy Policy for WordMaster Pro

**Last updated:** 2025

## Overview
WordMaster Pro ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension analyzes text locally on your device and does not transmit any user data to external servers.

## Data Collection and Usage

### What We Do Not Collect:
- **No text content**: The extension processes all text locally in your browser. Your documents, web pages, selections, and any analyzed text never leave your computer.
- **No personal information**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.
- **No browsing history**: The extension does not track your browsing history or monitor your web activity.

### What We Store Locally:
- **Settings and preferences**: Your extension settings (reading speed, theme preferences, etc.) are stored locally in your browser's storage.
- **Session data**: Temporary text selections may be stored in session storage for the context menu functionality, but this data is cleared when you close the browser.

### Permissions Explanation:
- **contextMenus**: To add the "Analyze Selected Text" option to right-click menus
- **activeTab**: To access the currently active tab only when you interact with the extension
- **storage**: To save your preferences and settings locally
- **scripting**: To optionally highlight keywords on web pages (only with user interaction)

## Data Security
All text analysis occurs entirely within your browser using JavaScript. No data is sent to external servers or third parties. Your privacy is maintained because the extension works offline and processes everything locally.

## Third-Party Services
The extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or external APIs. There are no advertisements or data sharing with third parties.

## Changes to This Policy
If we update this privacy policy, we will post the changes here and update the "Last updated" date.

## Contact
If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the Chrome Web Store developer contact form.

## Compliance
This extension complies with Chrome Web Store policies and Manifest V3 requirements for privacy and security.
